A nonprofit dedicated to inspiring appreciation and improvement of the environment through horticulture, education, and community stewardship. The Center cultivates urban green spaces, leads tree-planting and beautification programs across Wilmington, and offers classes, workshops, and signature events that bring gardeners of all ages together.
