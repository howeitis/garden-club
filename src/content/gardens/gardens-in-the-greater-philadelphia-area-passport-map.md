  America's Garden Capital is a partnership of the public gardens of the Philadelphia region, which has more of them within a short drive than almost anywhere else in the country. The passport turns visiting them into a collection: each garden has a page with its highlights, and a stamp or sticker to earn when you go.

  It makes a fine club outing, or a good gift for a new member.

America's Garden Capital brings together more than thirty public gardens, arboreta, and historic landscapes across the Philadelphia region, from southeastern Pennsylvania to northern Delaware and South Jersey. Its map and garden passport are a handy way to plan a season of visits.
