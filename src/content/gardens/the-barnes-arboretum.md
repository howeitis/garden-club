A 12-acre living masterpiece established in 1922 by Laura L. Barnes, featuring more than 2,500 species of woody and herbaceous plants. The collection includes a renowned fern garden, peony garden, rose garden, and significant specimens of magnolias, lilacs, and stewartias, all arranged with the same artistic sensibility that shaped the famed Barnes Foundation art collection.
