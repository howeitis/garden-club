A 12-acre living collection begun by Laura L. Barnes, with more than 2,500 kinds of woody and herbaceous plants — many of them rare. Its fern, peony, and lilac collections date from the early 1900s, and its stewartias, magnolias, and conifers include a number of Pennsylvania state champion trees, all arranged with the same artistic sensibility that shaped the famed Barnes Foundation art collection.
