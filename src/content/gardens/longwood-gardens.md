  Pierre S. du Pont bought the property in 1906 to save its grove of old trees from the sawmill, and spent the rest of his life turning it into a showplace of gardens, glasshouses, and fountains. His engineering enthusiasm still shows in the Main Fountain Garden, fully restored and reopened in 2017.

  The *Longwood Reimagined* project that opened in November 2024 added the West Conservatory, whose glass peaks seem to float on a reflecting pool, and a courtyard for one of the country's finest bonsai collections.

One of the world's great horticultural display gardens, Longwood spans more than 1,000 acres of fountains, formal gardens, woodland, and meadow. Its conservatory district reopened in 2024 with a new West Conservatory and Bonsai Courtyard, and the year-round calendar of seasonal displays and illuminated fountain shows gives a reason to come back every season.
