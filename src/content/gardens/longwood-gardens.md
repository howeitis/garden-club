One of the world's premier horticultural display gardens, Longwood spans over 1,000 acres and features spectacular fountains, a conservatory with 4,600 plant species, extensive outdoor gardens, and a renowned meadow garden. The gardens offer year-round programming, seasonal displays, and breathtaking illuminated fountain performances.
