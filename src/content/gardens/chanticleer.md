  Chanticleer was built in 1912 as the country home of the Rosengarten family, and opened as a public garden in 1993. Its gardeners are also its designers: each is responsible for an area and changes it year to year, so the garden never stands still.

  The Ruin Garden is its best-known room. After the Minder House was taken down in 1999, its footprint became a series of roofless rooms with pools and fountains — an imagined ruin that is now one of the most photographed gardens in the region.

A pleasure garden of unrivaled imagination, Chanticleer is a former estate garden often described as one of the most romantic and creative public gardens in America. Each area is designed and tended by its own horticulturist, resulting in a constantly evolving tapestry of bold plant combinations, hand-crafted furniture, and inventive seasonal displays.
