A pleasure garden of unrivaled imagination, Chanticleer is a 48-acre estate garden often described as one of the most romantic and creative public gardens in America. Each area is designed and tended by a single horticulturist, resulting in a constantly evolving tapestry of bold plant combinations, hand-crafted furniture, and inventive seasonal displays.
