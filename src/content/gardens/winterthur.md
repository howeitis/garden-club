Set on nearly 1,000 acres in the Brandywine Valley, Winterthur's naturalistic garden is one of the finest in North America. Designed by Henry Francis du Pont, the garden features sweeping vistas, a stunning March Bank of early spring bulbs, native azaleas, and a renowned collection of rare and unusual plants arranged to look as though nature placed them.
