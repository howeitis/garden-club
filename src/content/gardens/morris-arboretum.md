The official arboretum of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania and a beloved 92-acre living museum operated by the University of Pennsylvania. Morris Arboretum features more than 13,000 labeled plants, a Victorian-era log cabin, a swan pond, the famous Out on a Limb tree adventure canopy walk, and one of the finest collections of mature trees in the region.
