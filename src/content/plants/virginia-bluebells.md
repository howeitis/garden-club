---
name: Virginia Bluebells
scientificName: Mertensia virginica
type: native
bloom: March – May
image: /bluebells.webp
order: 30
---

A beloved spring ephemeral wildflower producing loose clusters of pendulous, trumpet-shaped flowers — pink in bud, opening to clear sky blue. Thriving in moist, rich woodland soils along streams throughout the Brandywine Valley, it naturalizes beautifully.
