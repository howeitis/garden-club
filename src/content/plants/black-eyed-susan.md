---
name: Black-eyed Susan
scientificName: Rudbeckia hirta
type: native
bloom: June – September
image: /blackeyedsusan.webp
order: 40
---

A cheerful, drought-tolerant wildflower with bright golden-yellow petals surrounding a dark brown central cone. It thrives in full sun and well-drained Piedmont soils, self-sows readily, and provides long-lasting summer color while attracting pollinators.
