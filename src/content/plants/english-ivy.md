---
name: English Ivy
scientificName: Hedera helix
type: invasive
image: /englishivy.webp
order: 10
---

Introduced to the U.S. in the 1700s as an ornamental groundcover. It spreads rapidly, outcompetes native vegetation, and kills trees by creating dense, heavy, evergreen mats that choke out light and cause wind-shear damage.
