  Redbud blooms on bare wood — along twigs, branches and even the trunk — weeks before its leaves appear, which is why a redbud in flower looks like a pink haze at the edge of the woods. It's a member of the pea family, and the flowers are edible, with a slightly tart taste that brightens a spring salad.

  Redbud is a natural understory tree, happiest at the sunny edge of taller ones, and its modest size suits most home gardens. Plant it young: it doesn't take kindly to being moved once established.

A stunning small understory tree that bursts into bloom in early spring, covering bare branches with clusters of rosy pink flowers before heart-shaped leaves emerge. One of the earliest and showiest spring-flowering trees, it provides critical early-season nectar for native bees.
