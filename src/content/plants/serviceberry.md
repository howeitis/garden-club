more: |-
  Serviceberry goes by many names. *Shadbush* and *shadblow* come from its flowers opening just as shad swim up East Coast rivers to spawn; *Juneberry* from its fruit. It's a small tree for all four seasons — spring flowers, summer fruit, fall color, and smooth gray bark in winter — and a fine native replacement for Callery pear.

  The fruit is sweet and edible, good fresh or in pies and jam, though you'll need to pick quickly to beat the birds.
---

One of the first native trees to flower, covering bare branches with clouds of white blossoms in early spring. Sweet, blueberry-like fruit follows in June, and the leaves turn orange and red in fall.
