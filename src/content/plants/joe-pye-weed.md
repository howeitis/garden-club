more: |-
  Sweet Joe-Pye weed is the woodland-edge member of its family, happier in ordinary garden soil than its cousins of wet meadows, and its flowers carry a faint scent of vanilla. The domed flower heads can reach a foot or more across and draw clouds of butterflies at the end of summer, when many other flowers have finished.

  Give it the back of the border and room to stand tall. If seven feet is too much, cut the stems back by about half in late spring; the plant will branch, bloom a little later and stay shorter.

A tall, stately native perennial producing large, vanilla-scented domes of dusty pink-purple flowers. A powerhouse pollinator plant that acts as a magnet for butterflies and bees along stream edges and woodland borders.
