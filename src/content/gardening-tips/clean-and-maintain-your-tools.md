---
title: Clean and Maintain Your Tools
section: rhythms
frequency: Monthly (and immediately after pruning diseased plants)
image: /gardentools.webp
order: 60
---

Dull blades crush plant stems rather than making clean cuts, leaving jagged wounds that are highly vulnerable to pathogens. Additionally, sap and dirt on tools can harbor and transmit soil-borne bacterial and fungal diseases between plants. Regular cleaning, oiling, and sharpening protect the garden's overall health.
