---
title: Harvest Often to Encourage Yields
section: rhythms
frequency: Every 2–3 Days (during peak harvest season)
image: /harvest.webp
order: 40
---

For fruiting plants like beans, cucumbers, and tomatoes, leaving mature fruit on the vine signals to the plant that its reproductive cycle is complete, slowing further flowering. Frequent harvesting keeps the plant in active production and ensures produce is picked at peak flavor.
