---
title: Deadhead for Continuous Blooms
section: rhythms
frequency: Weekly to Bi-weekly (during the growing season)
image: /deadhead.webp
order: 30
---

When flowers fade and begin to set seed, hormonal signals direct the plant's energy into reproduction. Removing spent blooms (deadheading) interrupts this process, redirecting energy back into vegetative growth and the production of new flower buds.
