Integrated Pest Management (IPM) relies heavily on early detection. Regularly inspecting the undersides of leaves, stems, and soil helps catch outbreaks—like aphids or fungal spores—before they spread. Early intervention allows for mechanical controls (like hand-picking or a blast of water) rather than chemical interventions.
