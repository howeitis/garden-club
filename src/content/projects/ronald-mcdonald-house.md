GGGC members gather at the Ronald McDonald House to prepare for the Christmas Season by decorating a tree with Noah’s Ark ornaments that members have made by hand. Occasionally some animals get injured or wander off, so they are repaired and replaced. Under the tree is a wooden ark with Noah, his family and the animals.
