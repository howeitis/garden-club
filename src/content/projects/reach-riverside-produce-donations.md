Part of NGC’s 2023-25 PLANT AMERICA — FEED AMERICA program designed to help fight food insecurity. GGGC continues to implement this program by donating food to the two community refrigerators operating in the Riverside neighborhood of downtown Wilmington. Fresh produce is collected at meetings and delivered to refrigerators in front of the Kingswood Community Senior Center and the REACH Riverside Warehouse for Teens.
