GGGC installed the Blue Star Memorial Marker at the Wilmington VA Medical Center in 2014. Dedication was on Veterans’ Day, Nov. 10, that year. The site continues to be maintained with plantings. Each Memorial Day and Veterans’ Day, GGGC members gather, along with hospital residents and staff, to celebrate in the hospital’s Community Room. This way more veterans, especially those in wheelchairs, can attend the ceremony. A marker is displayed and a wreath is presented.
