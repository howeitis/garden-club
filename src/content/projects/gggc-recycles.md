This club activity promotes recycling as a civic duty and an environmental responsibility. The club collects Styrofoam, light bulbs, and batteries, which are delivered to the Delaware Solid Waste Authority. GGGC members take #2, 4, 5 plastic to Eco Plastics Products of DE. The goal is to collect enough plastic to earn an ECOP bench that can be donated to a public garden. Members are kept informed on dates and locations for recycling electronic goods and household hazardous waste. This is a DFGC award-winning activity.
