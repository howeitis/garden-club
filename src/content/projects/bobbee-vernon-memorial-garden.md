In 2012, the club installed a small garden in front of the Delaware Museum of Nature & Science in memory of longtime museum member and two-time GGGC President, Bobbee Vernon. It is designed to be of interest to visitors and touchable to children; to enhance the space; to be low maintenance; and to support the purpose of the museum. Delaware stone and petrified wood are placed among the plantings. Four garden club members provide maintenance for this garden.
