This is the President’s Project to honor the memory of Barbara Noseworthy who chaired GGGC’s Blue Star Marker Project from its inception in 2014. GGGC has long been unable to routinely work in that garden area due to the red-tape involved with doing work on government property. However, there has been some indication that may change. We are hopeful that we can proceed with the necessary clean-up and new plantings.
