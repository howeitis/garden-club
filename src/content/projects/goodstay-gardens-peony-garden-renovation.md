This project marks a new opportunity for Garden Gate to continue its support of Goodstay Gardens — one of Wilmington’s best-kept secrets. The focus for 2025-2026 will be on enhancing the peony garden. GGGC will assist with Goodstay’s plans to replace several of the aging tree peonies along the Main Walk and to create a new display featuring Itoh (intersectional) peonies. To support this effort, Garden Gate has allocated $1,000. In addition, the club will coordinate with Goodstay to schedule a visit for club members to enjoy the peony garden at its peak bloom.
